# ResumeSetu landing page

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Help a job seeker review the fit between their existing experience and a specific role, then choose the next application step with confidence grounded in their documents.

**OWN-WORLD:** Aegean Ledger: limestone paper, deep Aegean ink, bronze and olive details, classical serif headlines, a Parthenon line illustration, a laurel crown, a small Zeus-like lightning mark, and a quiet Greek meander rule. Apply the same Greek classic language to the workspace, admin, navigation, and dialogs while preserving honest product facts.

**STORY:** Keep the product facts, routes, and actions. Make the words feel written for a person applying to jobs: short labels, clear next steps, and plain explanations. Let the document preview prove the comparison use case; keep claims honest and reviewable.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-height editorial hero with a laurel crown above the existing ResumeSetu headline and a bronze Parthenon illustration below the copy. A lightning mark accents the primary action and a fine meander rule closes the hero. Keep the resume-to-role comparison immediately below as proof, with readable contrast and responsive spacing.

**FORM:** Code-led React implementation. Use limestone paper, Aegean navy, bronze, olive, DM Serif Display headlines, and Manrope body type across public and signed-in views. Light mode is the default; dark mode uses deep blue panels and warm ivory with bronze highlights. Keep theme persistence, keyboard quick-jump, Lenis cleanup, reduced-motion support, and all application routes/APIs/business logic. Avoid heavy dependencies and decorative clutter.

**FINISH:** Preserve content and operation, verify contrast, typography, responsive layout, keyboard focus, motion preferences, and the production build. Do not deploy or push unless asked.
