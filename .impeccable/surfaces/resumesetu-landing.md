**OWN-WORLD:** A nocturnal evidence studio: midnight ink, restrained purple/blue/teal aurora, oversized Space Grotesk headlines, and translucent glass panels that frame real product previews. Use warm, high-contrast text and a light theme toggle. Bento layouts organize the current landing content; avoid alarmist ATS language, invented proof, and ornamental glass without a clear grouping purpose.

**STORY:** State ResumeSetu's purpose and action immediately, show a clearly illustrative resume-to-role preview, then progress through current workflow, capabilities, exports, product facts, pricing, and FAQs. Preserve existing wording and working CTAs.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Keep the current product headline and scan action beside the sample comparison preview. Use the aurora as atmosphere, not as content; the preview remains the evidence.

**FORM:** Code-led React implementation. User-pinned elements: bento sections, liquid-glass cards, dark aurora with a persistent light/dark toggle, bold typography, micro-interactions, scroll reveals, active sticky navigation, a small neo-brutalist scan action, Ctrl/Cmd+K quick navigation, and optional restrained 3D tilt. Keep the existing React/Vite app and preserve APIs, authentication, routes, copy, and business logic. No new heavy libraries or external rasters. Seed reference: eaeabbc3529db124.

**FINISH:** Verify desktop and narrow viewport, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, contrast, theme persistence, and the command palette. Do not deploy or push.
