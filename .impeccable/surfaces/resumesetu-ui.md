# ResumeSetu UI surface brief

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make the resume-to-role comparison feel like a calm, evidence-led conversation: show what the candidate documented, what the role asks for, and what the candidate may want to review.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm paper, ink, evergreen, and a small clay accent. Keep the existing IBM Plex Sans, Space Grotesk, and JetBrains Mono families; use precise labels, open spacing, fine rules, and quiet paper panels. Remove the blue blueprint grid, heavy glass, and loud gradients.

**STORY:** A job seeker first sees the document-to-role comparison, then understands matched evidence and gaps, then chooses whether to scan, revise a source-grounded draft, or track the application. Repeat the distinction between fit and hiring outcome where scores appear.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim navigation bar sits above a spacious two-column hero. The left side carries the product thesis, free-scan action, sign-in expectation, and privacy cue. The right side shows a labeled illustrative resume-to-role preview with matched terms and one evidence gap. No company marks or candidate outcomes.

**FORM:** Code-led, direction option 1: editorial evidence desk. Signature interaction: selecting a sample skill reveals whether it is supported or a gap. Use no external raster until Pollinations is callable in this session and the chosen model’s commercial rights are verified. Seed: none (unavailable).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
